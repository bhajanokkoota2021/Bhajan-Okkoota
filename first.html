Competition Details

A State-Level Bhajan Competition is being organized by the Bhajan Mandaligala Okkootada Sansthe (Regd.), Mumbai.

Bhajan Mandalis from all 38 districts of Maharashtra will participate, with around 500 Bhajan Mandalis expected to compete. From these, 30 finalists will be selected.

🏆 Grand Prizes
  • 1st: ₹1,00,000/- and Trophy
  • 2nd: ₹75,000/- and Trophy
  • 3rd: ₹50,000/- and Trophy
  • 7 Consolation Prizes: ₹5,000/- each
Participation requires a clear understanding of the rules and guidelines.
