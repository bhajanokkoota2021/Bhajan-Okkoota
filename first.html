Competition Details

A State-Level Bhajan Competition is being organized by the Mandaligala Okkootada Sanstha (Regd.), Mumbai.

Bhajan Mandalis from all 36 districts of Maharashtra will participate, with around 500 Bhajan Mandalis expected to compete. From these, 50 finalists will be selected.

Grand Prizes

  • 1st: ₹1,00,000/- and Trophy
  • 2nd: ₹75,000/- and Trophy
  • 3rd: ₹50,000/- and Trophy
  • 7 Consolation Prizes: ₹5,000/- each

Additionally, there are 50 Special Prizes to be won by other participating Mandalis.

Participation requires a clear understanding of the rules and guidelines.
