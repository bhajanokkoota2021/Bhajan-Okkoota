Who We Are

About & Appeal

In the year 2021, during a Bhajana training camp held at Shri Kshetra Dharmasthala, two devotees from Mumbai city, Shri Nagesh Suvarna and Shri Ravindra Shanti, participated in the camp and gained valuable knowledge about disciplined living and the structured system of Bhajana, which includes melody (Raga), rhythm (Tala), tempo (Laya), and spiritual discipline. Inspired by this experience, they felt a strong responsibility to spread this knowledge among the Bhajana devotees of Mumbai and to motivate the younger generation towards the field of Bhajana and spirituality.

With this long-term vision, they approached Shri Dattatreya Durgambika Temple, one of the well-known religious centers of Mumbai, and met Dharmadarshi Dev B. Poojary, believing him to be the right person to lead this noble initiative. When the proposal for establishing the Maharashtra unit of Dharmasthala Shri Manjunatheshwara Bhajana Parishad Maharashtra Unit was presented, Shri Dev B. Poojary advised that more Bhajana devotees should be united and proper discussions should be held before implementing the vision.

Following his guidance, respected members including V.K. Suvarna Padubidri, Shri Sridhar Shetty, Shri Shantaram Putran, Shri Chandrahas Rai, Shri Vaman Suvarna, and Shri Kusha Poojary joined together for several rounds of discussions and meetings. As a result, an organization named Bhajana Mandaligala Okkootada Samsthe (Federation of Bhajana Mandalis) was formed with the aim of bringing together Tulu-Kannada Bhajana Mandalis across Mumbai and its suburban regions. The organization was divided into five zones for better coordination and was officially registered under the Maharashtra Charity Commissioner’s office as a Charitable Trust and Public Trust.

Today, many Bhajana Mandalis from all five zones have become registered members of the organization. The institution is actively conducting Bhajana training camps and encouraging especially the younger generation to participate in Bhajana and cultural activities. Senior Bhajana practitioners along with enthusiastic youth are working tirelessly towards this mission. Training camps are being conducted regularly across all five zones, with the core objective of ensuring that Bhajana becomes a part of every household. Apart from spiritual and Bhajana-related activities, the organization also aims to provide social, educational, and financial support services to the registered Bhajana devotees and their family members.

For any organization to grow and successfully implement its objectives, the support and contribution of donors and well-wishers are extremely essential. Therefore, with great hope, faith, and humility, we sincerely request religious thinkers, respected dignitaries, social organizations, and generous well-wishers to extend their support and encouragement towards this noble service initiative.
